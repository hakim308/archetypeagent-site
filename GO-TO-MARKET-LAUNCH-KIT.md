# Archetype Agent — Pre-launch go-to-market kit
Updated: 8 November 2026

## Launch decision

Do not take subscription payments yet. The public product is currently an early, no-login draft studio. It supports social post, promotion, customer-reply, 7-day schedule and 30-day plan drafts. It does not yet provide persistent workspaces, campaign execution, ecommerce integrations, analytics or paid subscription entitlements. Marketing must accurately describe what exists.

Primary beachhead for validation: English-speaking solo operators and small service businesses (1–10 people) who need consistent social content but do not have a marketing employee. Ecommerce merchants remain a second segment until catalogue and email integrations are built.

## Positioning for the current product

"An AI-assisted marketing draft studio for busy small-business owners. Create social posts, promotions, customer replies and practical content plans from your real business details — then review, edit and publish."

Avoid claims of guaranteed leads, revenue growth, automatic posting, campaign analytics, inbox automation or Shopify/WooCommerce integrations until these capabilities exist and are verified.

## Pilot offer

Invite 10–15 design partners to a 14-day guided pilot. Offer free access in exchange for a 20-minute onboarding interview and permission to collect product feedback. Do not collect payment in the pilot. Ask whether they would pay $19/month for a future Starter plan after seeing the workflow. Treat $19 as a pricing hypothesis, not a live offer.

Pilot success:
- 10 qualified businesses onboarded.
- 7 complete at least one AI-assisted task.
- 4 return for a second session within 7 days.
- 3 explicitly say they would pay $19/month and explain why.
- Track generation success, time-to-first-draft, edit rate, repeat usage, support issues and AI cost per session.

## Landing-page conversion improvements

Current CTA should remain "Try the free studio" while the product is free. Add a separate pilot interest CTA only after a secure form and privacy notice are implemented. Do not display the operator's PayPal email publicly. Keep the product preview honest and label AI beta accurately. Add clear limitations, support contact, privacy and terms pages before collecting any prospect data.

## 30-day organic acquisition plan

### Week 1 — Find the problem
- Build a list of 50 qualified small-business prospects from public business directories and relevant communities; obey each community's promotion rules.
- Contact 10 prospects individually per day with a short question, not a sales blast.
- Interview at least 5 owners about their weekly marketing bottleneck.
- Publish 3 educational posts: content consistency, turning service details into useful posts, and responding to customer enquiries.

### Week 2 — Demonstrate value
- Run 5 guided demos using the prospect's own non-sensitive business details.
- Publish 3 short screen-recorded demonstrations (no fabricated testimonials or results).
- Share a useful free 7-day content checklist in relevant small-business communities where promotion is permitted.
- Ask every tester what was confusing and what they would use weekly.

### Week 3 — Improve retention
- Fix the top 3 usability problems.
- Publish before/after examples using clearly labelled fictional sample businesses.
- Re-contact pilot users and measure second-session usage.
- Interview users who did not return to understand why.

### Week 4 — Pricing validation
- Show a simple proposed $19/month Starter concept to active testers.
- Ask for a clear purchase-intent signal; do not charge until legal and payment readiness are complete.
- Decide go/no-go based on pilot metrics and per-session AI cost.
- Only then scope accounts, saved workspaces, usage limits and subscription checkout.

## Channels and cadence

- Facebook groups: 2 helpful contributions per week, only where self-promotion is allowed.
- LinkedIn: 2 posts per week aimed at independent operators and small-business owners.
- Instagram: 2 short screen-recorded demos per week.
- TikTok: 2 concise product demonstrations per week; show the workflow, not generic AI imagery.
- Direct outreach: 10 carefully personalized messages per working day, respecting opt-outs and anti-spam rules.
- Website: one clear primary CTA; no fake counters, fabricated reviews or unsupported claims.

## Payment and compliance gate

Before accepting money:
1. Confirm the correct Algerian legal form and activity with a qualified local accountant/authority.
2. Confirm export-of-services invoicing, tax, banking and foreign-exchange handling with the bank.
3. Confirm PayPal seller onboarding and the account's ability to accept commercial website/API payments in Algeria.
4. Prefer a PayPal Business account for commercial sales; do not expose personal account details in page source or public marketing.
5. Test checkout and refund in sandbox/test mode first; use a low-value live test only after provider approval and legal readiness.
6. Implement signed, idempotent payment webhooks and server-side entitlements before any paid plan is advertised.

## Go/no-go

Proceed to paid launch only when:
- AI generation works reliably from the public site.
- Product limitations are disclosed.
- Legal and seller eligibility are confirmed.
- At least 10 pilot users have tried the product and the repeat-use / willingness-to-pay signals are credible.
- Usage limits and AI cost per account support a sustainable gross margin.
- Customer data, deletion, privacy, terms, cancellation and refund flows are implemented.
